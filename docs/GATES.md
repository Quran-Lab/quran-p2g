- `ours_match_wasl_*`: quran-p2g accepts the first word continuing the previous ayah, or the last word running into the next, in wasl (open edges); the benchmark lattice has ibtida and waqf edges only.

- `one_error_attributed_to_neighbouring_word`: one edit at a word boundary, flagged on the word on one side by quran-p2g and on the other side by the scorer.
- `ours_deviant_theirs_match` / `ours_match_other`: an alignment tie resolved differently (see the full report next to the text hypotheses).
