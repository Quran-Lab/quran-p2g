## Changes since 0.1.0

Every 0.1.0 G2 miss and G3 disagreement was put in one class: an attribution tie (a), a convention difference with the benchmark scorer (b), or a bug (c). The bugs are fixed (DESIGN.md sections 5 and 7); the ties and conventions are measured as described below, with the strict numbers kept.

| number | 0.1.0 | 0.1.1 strict | 0.1.1 with ties / conventions |
|---|---|---|---|
| G2 exact, tj3g | 99.87 % | 99.95 % | 100.00 % |
| G2 exact, tj3 | 99.75 % | 99.92 % | 100.00 % |
| G2 read-back, tj3g | 98.71 % | 98.88 % | 100.00 % (named rules) |
| G2 read-back, tj3 | 98.38 % | 98.58 % | 100.00 % (named rules) |
| G3 word flags vs scorer, V4 / v3.1 test | 99.94 / 99.86 % | 99.94 / 99.86 % | 100.00 / 100.00 % |
| G4 exact ayah, canonical | 97.07 % | 97.07 % (the ceiling) | 100.00 % up to identical text |

0.1.0 G2 misses: 93 (32 tj3g, 61 tj3): 63 were (c), a skip or repeat explained as a stop plus a word fragment, fixed in the aligner; 30 are (a), identical phone strings. 0.1.0 read-back differences: 491; after the speller and gate fixes every one left is forced by a named reading rule. G3: no (c); every disagreement is (b) or (a).

0.1.2 (DESIGN.md, last section): a word re-split after the alignment, so one wrong word is one word error (a symmetric test had a replaced word cost 18.11 % WER through the phones against 14.57 % as text), and three tajweed-level rules narrowed to where they apply (they hid a few real letter mistakes). Gate G5 is new. G2 stays at 100.00 % up to an equivalent attribution; its strict number moves to 99.79 % (tj3g) and 99.78 % (tj3) because the re-split can put an edit on a neighbouring word that gives the identical phones. G3 WER falls (the double charge is gone) while its word flags still agree with the benchmark's scorer.

0.1.3: extra speech (a word not in the ayah, a glued repeat) is written as words of its own; G5 adds several mistakes together (0.1.2 charged three adjacent mistakes 30.65 % through the phones against 33.63 % as text; 0.1.3: 17.33 % against 17.14 % on the G5 set).

- `wasl_edge` (b): quran-p2g accepts the last word running into the next ayah (or the first continuing the previous one) in wasl; the scorer's lattice has ibtida and waqf edges only.
- `repeat_item` (b): quran-p2g reports repeated material as an inserted repeat and leaves the words around it a match; the scorer absorbs it into a neighbouring word and flags that word.
- `path_legality` (b): the scorer passes a word whose phones are a legal form in some boundary state, even when that state does not agree with its neighbour (فَإِذَا with its waqf long vowel, then ٱنشَقَّتِ in wasl); quran-p2g requires the reading to be one legal path and flags it.
- `tie_in_scorer_cost` (a): the scorer's own cost gives quran-p2g's attribution exactly the scorer's optimum; the two differ only in which equally cheap path the scorer's backtrace picks (one error at a word boundary, a truncated clip whose leftover tokens the scorer spreads over the deleted words).
